Nominal scale:this scale classifies data into distinct categories that do not have intrinsic order.

Qualitative/Categorical data:

-Data is categorized based on name,label,quantities
-Categories are mutually exclusive
-No logical order among categories[No rank]

e.g- male ,female
colors- red, blue,pink

Ordinal scale:
Data is categorized in a way that it can be ranked or ordered

characteristics:
->data is categorized as per rank
->intervals are not equal

like degree vs rank
